KARMA — pay for a skill invocation with your own wallet

This page is a human-facing, additive payment path: connect a Casper wallet via CSPR.click, sign an EIP-712 TransferWithAuthorization for KARMA's X402SettlementToken, and get back a signed payment envelope. Signing here never touches KARMA's own agent keystore, and never calls any AgentSkillRegistry method (governance-gated or otherwise) — it only produces a signed authorization for the relay script (src/scripts/relay_casper_x402_envelope.ts) to verify and submit on-chain. Your wallet never needs testnet CSPR for gas — only KARMA's relayer key pays gas; your signature is what authorizes the transfer (CEP-3009 / EIP-3009).
